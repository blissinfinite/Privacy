1. Introduction
Welcome to Dojo Dost. This Privacy Policy describes how [Please Insert Your Legal Company Name Here] ("Dojo Dost", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information in connection with our AI-powered marketing and sales automation platform (the "Service"), which is provided to martial arts and fitness facilities (our "Customers" or "Gyms").

This policy is designed to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

2. Our Role: Data Fiduciary and Data Processor
Under the DPDP Act, our role changes depending on the data being processed:

Data Fiduciary: When we collect and process the personal data of our Customers (Gym Owners and their staff) for purposes like account creation, billing, and providing our service, we are the Data Fiduciary.

Data Processor: When we process the personal data of your Leads and Members (your "End-Users") on your behalf through our platform (e.g., via the AI Sales Agent, CRM, or analytics), you (the Gym) are the Data Fiduciary, and we are the Data Processor.

As the Data Fiduciary for your End-Users, you are responsible for obtaining their lawful consent to collect and process their personal data using our Service.

3. Information We Collect as a Data Fiduciary (From Our Customers)
We collect the following personal data directly from you, our Customer:

Account Information: Your name, your gym's name, email address, phone number, and password.

Billing Information: Payment details (such as credit card information or UPI ID) and billing address, processed by our third-party payment gateways.

Gym Operations Data: Information you provide to configure the Service, such as your gym's location, class schedules, pricing, staff information, and amenities.

Integration Data: Authentication tokens or credentials for third-party services you choose to connect, such as Google Calendar.

4. Information We Process as a Data Processor (On Behalf of Our Customers)
We collect and process the following data from your End-Users (Leads and Members) solely on your behalf and at your direction:

Lead Contact Information: Name, phone number (especially for WhatsApp), email address, and social media handles (from Instagram and Facebook Messenger).

Lead Conversation Data: The content of conversations between your Leads and our AI Sales Agent, which may include their expressed interests, fitness goals, and experience level.

Scheduling Information: Dates and times for trial classes or appointments booked through the platform.

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Data: Status of leads, communication history, and other notes you add about your End-Users.

[Future Feature] Payment Data: If you use our features to collect trial deposits or membership fees, we will process payment information from your End-Users via our integrated payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay, PayU, Paytm).

[Future Feature] Member Behavioral Data: For features like churn analysis, we will process data from your systems, such as member attendance frequency, class booking history, and payment patterns.

5. How We Use Information
As a Data Fiduciary (For Our Customers):

To provide, maintain, and improve our Service.

To create and manage your account and process your subscription payments.

To provide customer support and communicate with you about service updates, security alerts, and billing.

To market new features or services to you (our Customer).

As a Data Processor (For Your End-Users):

To act on your behalf to power the AI Sales Agent, enabling it to respond to inquiries, answer questions, and book trials.

To populate your centralized lead inbox and CRM.

To send automated follow-ups and reminders via WhatsApp, SMS, or email as configured by you.

To generate analytics and reports for you about your lead conversion and (in the future) member retention.

We will never use your End-Users' personal data to market our services to them directly, nor will we sell this data to any third party.

6. How We Share Information
We do not share personal data except in the following limited circumstances:

With Third-Party Service Providers: We engage third parties to help us operate our Service. These providers are our Data Processors and are contractually bound to protect your data.

AI & LLM Providers: To power our AI Sales Agent, we securely send conversation data to third-party Large Language Model (LLM) providers via their enterprise APIs. Our agreements with these providers prohibit them from using this data to train their models or for any purpose other than providing the service to us.

Communication Platforms: With Meta (for WhatsApp Business API, Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger) to enable message delivery.

Payment Gateways: With providers like Razorpay, PayU, and Paytm to process your subscription payments and any End-User payments you collect.

Cloud Hosting: With cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud) to host our platform and store data.

Other Integrations: With services you connect, like Google Calendar.

At Your Direction: We share your End-Users' data with you, the Gym, as the Data Fiduciary.

For Legal Reasons: If required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our Customers or the public.

In a Business Transfer: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

7. Data Security and Retention
We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect the data we process from unauthorized access, breach, or destruction.

We retain your Customer (Gym) data for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for legal or auditing purposes.

We retain your End-User (Lead/Member) data for as long as you instruct us to as part of our role as a Data Processor. When you delete a lead or member from your account, we will delete their personal data from our systems in accordance with our data deletion policies.

9. Children's Privacy
The DPDP Act provides specific protections for the data of children (defined as individuals under 18 years of age). Our Service is not intended for our Customers to knowingly collect personal data from children without verifiable parental consent. As the Data Fiduciary, you (the Gym) are responsible for obtaining such consent before processing a child's data.
